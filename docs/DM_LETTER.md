# The DM Letter

A tool for a player running the story as its dungeon master: a letter from one NPC to another, sent when they choose, with what they choose. It goes out as an ordinary letter between NPCs. It's delivered off-screen, can be stolen and read, and its recipient reads it and may reply like any other ([NPC_TO_NPC.md](NPC_TO_NPC.md)). Code: `src/DmLetter.cpp`, the page in `Letters::SheetText`, the prompt `physical_letters/dm_letter.prompt`. Other mods send the same letters through `DmLetter::Send` ([API.md](API.md#sending)): no HUD notices, and -1 hours for the travel time.

**Status:** built 2026-10-29 (user's design), not yet run in game. Needs Ink & Quill's API **version 3** (`IQ_WRITE_FREE`), which all of Physical Letters now requires (user, 2026-10-31: no fallback to version 2).

## The item

`PhysicalLettersDMLetter` (`0x8BB`): a book with the parchment's look, value 0, weight 0, no keywords, so no merchant buys it and the hand-in and postage menus don't list it. Its name and its item card ("Send a letter from anyone to anyone.") come from the locale (`DmLetter`, `DmLetterCard`, [LOCALIZATION.md](LOCALIZATION.md)): the ESP's are English, the name is set at `kDataLoaded`, and the card is answered by the item card hook (`DmLetter::CardFor`, as letters' cards are). The player gets it from the MCM: General page, "DM Letter" section, "Add the DM Letter" (the native `AddDmLetter`). It adds one if the player carries none, and the option then shows "Added", "Already in your inventory" or "Can't be used: see PhysicalLetters.log" (no Ink & Quill, or its writing off, or the record missing from the ESP; the log says which).

It's an Ink & Quill blank (`RegisterBlank`): reading it from the inventory begins writing at once, as parchment does. The session's way is `IQ_WRITE_FREE`: no quill, ink or blood, whatever Ink & Quill's settings. Every save is answered with `ReplySave` (no book), so **it's never used up**: the item stays, its page blank again for the next letter.

## The sheet

Seven runs while "From:" names a writer, six once Forgery is ticked (no Transform box), five with no writer (only the Package box), in the letter's handwriting:

```
From: Lydia, 6391 Whiterun
To: Adrianne Avenicci, 2204 Whiterun
Delivery (hours): 0
Package [ ]   Transform [x]   Forgery [ ]

The text.
```

- **From and To:** the same lines as the player's letters ([WRITING.md](WRITING.md#the-recipient)): Ink & Quill's suggestions as you type (`Writing::Suggest`), and a person by their name and, if several share it, the whole address. "To:" is resolved as a letter's (`Recipients::Resolve`), or is **the player** when it's their name (`DmLetter::ResolveTo`; there are no suggestions for it, since the lookup never lists the player), "From:" as a forgery's sender (`Recipients::ResolveSender`, in `DmLetter::ResolveFrom`): a person alive, not the recipient, writes it; **empty** is an unsigned letter and **a name that's nobody's** ("A friend", "The Dark Brotherhood") a signed one, as on the player's letters (user, 2026-10-31; until then both were refused). A real person's name without the address it needs is refused with the "To:" line's messages, as on the player's letters, and keeps the Transform line locked. The player's own name is refused ("A DM Letter can't be from you."). Enter goes on to the next line.
- **Delivery (hours):** the game hours until it's delivered; 0 (the default) or empty is now. Only digits stay, at most 4 (up to 9999). It replaces the travel time a scheduled letter would take. Enter goes on to the text.
- **Package:** always there, first on the boxes' line (user, 2026-11-01: dispatching several letters without packages shouldn't mean answering a question each time). Ticked, saving closes the book and the inventory and opens the packing chest; the letter goes once it's closed, with what was put in ([PACKAGES.md](PACKAGES.md#packing)). Unticked, the letter goes at once and the book stays open.
- **The boxes:** Transform and Forgery, after Package on the same line, there only while "From:" names a writer. With no writer (an empty or made-up "From:", or a name still being typed) their part of the line is locked and greyed out, with no boxes (the Package box stays), so nobody can be asked to transform it (user, 2026-10-31). Ticking Forgery greys out and locks Transform alone, its box gone and cleared: a forgery isn't transformed (user, 2026-10-31); clearing Forgery brings it back. `OnChange` reloads the page whenever the boxes change (`DmLetter::Layout`), and a box that goes away is cleared. While they're there, each is an empty run right after its locked mark (`[x` or `[ `, then a locked `]`), ticked and cleared on its own: the caret has one place in a box, so Left and Right step over it as one thing (until 2026-10-31 the mark was the run, and the caret stopped on either side of the x). Anything typed or pasted there ticks it if it was clear and clears it if it was ticked (`OnChange` reloads the page), so nothing else can be typed on that line (user, 2026-10-29). Backspace and Delete do nothing there: the characters around an empty run are locked. Moving the caret into it changes nothing. The save reads the boxes from the page's state, not their runs.
- **The text:** unticked, it's the letter, word for word. Ticked, it's notes, and the writer puts them in their own words (below). Left empty, the writer chooses what to write about, whatever the box says (user, 2026-10-31); with no writer, or with Forgery ticked, that's refused ("Nothing is written in the letter yet.").

After a send, every line is cleared (user, 2026-10-30): the next letter starts on a blank page, with 0 hours and the box clear. Nothing is kept between sends, in memory or in the save.

## Sending

With the Package box ticked, the letter goes out once the packing chest is closed, with what was packed; a letter that can't be made then is a notice, its package given back. Every outcome is a corner notification (top left, as Papyrus's `Debug.Notification`: `SendHUDMessage::ShowHUDMessage`). The save is accepted at once and the page cleared; the letter is made then, or once the packing chest is closed when the Package box is ticked (until 2026-10-31 a letter sent as written was made first, so a failure kept the text on the page). Saving the page sends the letter, once these checks pass (each refusal is a message on the page, and writing goes on): letters are ready after a load, "To:" names one person, "From:" is empty, a name or one person (not the player), and there's text unless a writer will write it.

- **As written:** the letter is created at once (author the writer, `dispatched` set in LetterDB, [PERSISTENCE.md](PERSISTENCE.md)), queued to the recipient after the hours given (`Transit::QueueToNpc`) and put straight into their inventory then, never held for the courier even when the recipient is in the player's town (`Transit::Deliver`; until 2026-10-30 it waited for the courier like any letter, and a letter sent with 0 hours took minutes to arrive), and the writer gets the same memory of writing it as a scheduled writer ("I wrote a letter to X.", then the letter; `NpcToNpc::RememberWriting`). No LLM call. A HUD notice: "Lydia's letter to Adrianne is sent: delivered now."
- **Unsigned or signed, with no writer:** as written, always (no Transform). The letter has no author (`author_uuid` empty) and is `anonymous` or carries the name in `signed_name`, so its recipient reads it as they would the player's unsigned or signed letter ([FORGERY.md](FORGERY.md#reading-it)): "The letter is unsigned" or the name with no profile, nobody as the memory's related actor, and no reply, since there's nobody to write to (`Transit::CanReplyTo` checks that before it treats a letter as one between NPCs, which a letter with no author would pass). Nobody remembers writing it. The notice: "The unsigned letter to Adrianne is sent: …" or "The letter signed "A friend" to Adrianne is sent: …". A writer's letters list (`NpcToNpc::LetterLines`) calls it "had an unsigned letter" or names the signature. It has no route, so the road courier never carries it.
- **Transformed, or no text:** one call on the default model, `physical_letters/dm_letter.prompt`, with the writer's full profile, the recipient's public one, the notes, the writer's newest memories (`[NpcToNpc] MemoriesPerWriter`) and their earlier letters to each other. It writes the letter in the writer's voice, keeping every fact, name, place and request the notes give, plus their memory, emotion and importance. With no notes, the prompt has the writer choose the subject from their connection, memories, earlier letters and own life, as a scheduled letter would (`write_letter.prompt`'s reasons to write). Nothing decides whether they write: the letter is the DM's call (user, 2026-10-29). The HUD says "Lydia is writing to Adrianne. It goes out once written."; once the answer comes, the letter goes out as above, with the call's memory, and the hours count from then. A failed call, or one with no letter, sends nothing: "Lydia's letter to Adrianne couldn't be sent. See PhysicalLetters.log." An answer that arrives after a load is dropped. SkyrimNet drops a cancelled call without calling back, so a writer with no answer after 5 minutes has failed (`DmLetter::Tick`, on the heartbeat): "No answer … given up" in the log, the failure notice, and a late answer is dropped. The page was cleared at the save, so every failure logs the notes in full, to be written again.
- **To the player:** the same letter an NPC writes to them, from the writer, unsigned or signed (its item name is "Letter from …" with the name it bears, or plain "Letter" unsigned). Due after more than 0 hours, it goes to the courier like any NPC letter to the player (`Transit::QueueToPlayer`), so it arrives when he finds them; at 0 it's put straight into their inventory (user, 2026-10-31). The writer remembers writing it, as for any recipient. Notices: "Lydia's letter to you is in your inventory." or "Lydia's letter to you is sent: it reaches the courier in 5 hours."
- **Forgery** (user, 2026-10-31): the letter is in the writer's name but they didn't write it, and it always passes for theirs. It's stored as the player's forgeries are ([FORGERY.md](FORGERY.md)), with no author at all: the person named in `signed_uuid`, `signed_name` and `signed_address`, `forgery` 1 (passes), and nobody remembers writing it. Its reader reads it as that person's letter: their profile, their earlier letters, the memory's related actor. A reply goes to the person named under the player's forgeries' rules (`[NpcLetters] ForgeryReplies`, the forgery as letter 1 of the thread), and they read a reply to a letter they never wrote. It's always sent as written: Forgery takes the Transform box away, and an empty text is refused. Notice: "The forged letter from Lydia to Adrianne is sent: …".

## After it's sent

What the recipient does is theirs: they read it like any letter between NPCs and may reply, within the thread's `MaxLettersPerThread` ([NPC_TO_NPC.md](NPC_TO_NPC.md#keeping-it-bounded)). The schedule's own limits don't apply to sending (user, 2026-10-29): not the open threads limit, not the pair cooldown, not "lives in another area", not "already in a thread". Its thread doesn't count toward `MaxOpenThreads` (`NpcToNpc::OpenThreads` skips a thread whose first letter is `dispatched`), but its two NPCs are busy in it like any other, so the schedule doesn't start another thread with either while it's open.

## Logs
